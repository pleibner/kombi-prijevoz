# GEO audit: kombi-transport.com

**Date:** 2 October 2026
**Audited build:** v1.2.0 (commit `88f6487`, deployed to GitHub Pages on 2 October 2026 at 20:22 UTC)
**Market:** Zagreb and surroundings, Croatian-language site

> **Score: 43 / 100.** AI crawlers can read the site easily. Every route is prerendered to static HTML, robots.txt is open, and no CDN blocks bots. The problem is what they find. The service pages offer almost no facts an answer engine can quote, several pages contradict each other, and the brand barely exists outside its own domain. AI engines have little evidence that "Kombi Transport" at Trg Ivana Kukuljevića 5 is a distinct, reviewed business.

## How this was audited

- The `/geo` command isn't installed in this session, so this is a manual audit with the same coverage: crawler access, structured data, citability, trust and off-site entity presence.
- The sandbox network policy blocks requests to `kombi-transport.com`, so the live site couldn't be fetched. Instead I built the deployed commit with `vite-ssg` and inspected all 20 prerendered HTML files. Tag `1.2.0` is `HEAD`, and its deploy run succeeded. DNS confirms the domain points directly at GitHub Pages: `185.199.108–111.153`, with `www` CNAMEd to `pleibner.github.io`.
- I checked off-site presence with web search. That is a single US-region index, so treat it as directional, not as Google.hr or Bing results.
- **Not verified:**
  - live response headers and the HTTPS redirect
  - Google Business Profile and Bing Places
  - real answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Google AI Overviews
- The redesign went live today, so search and AI indexes still reflect v1.1.0. Re-check visibility in 3–4 weeks.

## What already works

- **Every route is prerendered.** `vite-ssg` emits full HTML for all 19 public routes. Most AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot) don't execute JavaScript, and many Vue and React sites show them an empty shell. This one doesn't.
- **Nothing blocks AI crawlers.** robots.txt is `User-agent: * / Allow: /`, and GitHub Pages doesn't filter bots, so OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity, Google and Bing crawlers all get through.
- **Every indexable page has a unique title, meta description and self-referencing canonical.** unhead correctly replaces the template's homepage values.
- **The homepage is quotable.** It states "od 25 € po satu", "dodatni radnik 10 € po satu", "selidba stana od 150 €", "odvoz glomaznog otpada od 60 €", "izvan Zagreba 0,90 € po kilometru", "svaki dan 08–20 h", "unutar 50 km od Zagreba" and "R1 račun na zahtjev". These are exactly the kinds of facts answer engines lift.
- **The FAQ gives direct answers** and has FAQPage JSON-LD. The HTML is semantic: `<main>`, `<nav>`, breadcrumbs, `<details>`.
- **Name, address and phone are identical everywhere on the site.**

## 1. Technical

### 1.1 Every page declares itself English (High, 5 minutes)

`index.html:2` says `<html lang="hr">`, but all 20 deployed pages render `<html lang="en">`. unhead's server renderer injects a default `htmlAttrs: { lang: "en" }` (`node_modules/unhead/dist/server.mjs`) that overrides the template.

- Bing uses `lang` as a language signal, and ChatGPT search and Copilot draw on Bing's index. Google mostly ignores it.
- Screen readers will read the Croatian text with English pronunciation.

The fix below was tested on a full build, and every page then emitted `lang="hr"`:

```ts
// src/App.vue, inside <script setup>
import { useHead } from '@unhead/vue'

useHead({ htmlAttrs: { lang: 'hr' } })
```

### 1.2 Preview tags point at the homepage on every page (Medium)

`og:url` (`index.html:26`), `twitter:title` (`index.html:31`) and `twitter:description` are hard-coded in the template and never overridden. All 18 subpages therefore share the homepage's values. `/kako-do-nas` and `/404` also inherit the homepage's `og:title` and `og:description`.

There is no `og:image` anywhere, so links shared in chat apps and AI answer cards render without a picture.

To fix it:

1. Remove the static OG and Twitter tags from `index.html`.
2. Add a `usePageMeta({ title, description, path })` composable that sets title, description, canonical, `og:*` and `twitter:*` together. It also replaces the ~30 lines of `useHead` copied into each of the 20 views.
3. Add a 1200×630 `og:image`, such as a photo of the van with the logo.

### 1.3 Stale sitemap dates (Low)

`public/sitemap.xml` gives `2024-11-09` as `lastmod` for 14 of its 19 URLs, although today's release changed every page. Generate the sitemap from the router at build time, or update the dates with each release. `changefreq` and `priority` are ignored by Google and can be removed.

### 1.4 No llms.txt (Low, optional)

A `public/llms.txt` takes about 15 minutes. It's markdown covering who you are, services, prices, service area, hours, contact details and links to key pages. No major AI engine has confirmed it reads the file, so treat it as cheap insurance rather than a ranking lever.

### 1.5 Minor

- The titles of 15 of the 19 indexable pages omit the brand, e.g. "Dostava bijele tehnike - Transport uređaja". Append `| Kombi Transport` consistently to tie each service to the brand.
- The 404 page has a canonical pointing at the homepage as well as `noindex`. Drop the canonical.

## 2. Structured data

**Current state:** one `LocalBusiness` block on every page (`index.html:39-108`), plus `FAQPage` on the homepage.

**Issues:**

- `@type: LocalBusiness` is generic. schema.org has a specific `MovingCompany` type.
- The schema says `"paymentAccepted": "Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer"` (`index.html:104`). The FAQ says "Gotovinom ili internet bankarstvom" (`src/data/faq.ts:20`). If cards aren't accepted, remove them from the schema.
- `areaServed` lists only Zagreb. The site names eight towns (`serviceAreas` in `src/data/site.ts`) and offers all of Croatia by arrangement.
- The offer catalogue lists three generic services, omits waste removal, and has no prices, even though the page publishes them.
- Missing properties:
  - `@id`, so every page can reference one entity
  - `logo` and `image`
  - `sameAs`, linking to Facebook and the Google Business Profile
  - `email` and `hasMap`
  - `legalName` and `taxID` (OIB)
- Service pages have no `Service` markup, and no `BreadcrumbList` despite rendering a visible breadcrumb.

**Testimonials:** don't mark them up as `Review` or `AggregateRating`. Google won't show review stars for reviews a business publishes about itself, so the markup gains nothing. The reviews that count live on the Google Business Profile.

## 3. Content citability

Answer engines assemble responses from short, self-contained passages containing specific facts. Searches for Zagreb van-transport questions show what they quote from competitors:

- "20 € po satu (samo vozač) ili 30 € po satu (vozač pomaže), minimalno 2 sata" (premium-prijevoz.com.hr)
- "najveći kombi na tržištu, tovarni prostor 17,5 m³, 15 godina rada" (Kombi prijevoz Fran, on preporuka.hr)
- "UNIQA osiguranje do 10.000 €, 25 godina iskustva" (selidbe-marijo.hr)
- per-hour price ranges from competitors' dedicated price pages (results included brk-selidbe.hr, kombi-prijevoz.hr, selidbe-zagreb.hr)
- the City of Zagreb's free bulky-waste pickup quota (zagreb.hr)

Kombi Transport's service pages contain none of this. In the deployed HTML:

| Page | Unique copy (words) | Prices | Concrete facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| /kombi-prijevoz | 137 | none | none; its "30+ godina" conflicts with the homepage |
| /kombi-selidbe | 138 | none | none |
| /kombi-dostava | 118 | none | vague "specijalizirana vozila" with city-centre access |
| /odvoz-otpada | 133 | none | none |
| /odvoz-glomaznog-otpada | 130 | none | none |
| /povoljan-kombi-prijevoz | 194 | none; says "odbacili smo rigidne cjenike" | none |
| 10 other service pages | 132–197 | none | none |
| /kako-do-nas | 49 | none | the address only; the rest is a map iframe |

Most service pages open with scene-setting filler, such as "U današnjem užurbanom životu, vrijeme je najvrjednija valuta." or "Zamislite da ste kupili…". None gives a price or a typical duration. Asked "koliko košta selidba garsonijere u Zagrebu?", an engine finds nothing on the moving pages to quote. The homepage's "Selidba stana od 150 €" card is the only place that price appears.

### Recommendations

1. **Open every service page with the answer.** The first two or three sentences should say what, where, from what price, what's included and how fast. This example for `/kombi-selidbe` uses only facts already on the homepage:

   > Selidbu garsonijere ili jednosobnog stana unutar Zagreba radimo od 150 €: kombi i dva radnika, nošenje, utovar, prijevoz i istovar. Kombi s vozačem koji pomaže pri utovaru stoji od 25 € po satu, a svaki dodatni radnik 10 € po satu. Radimo svaki dan od 8 do 20 h, u Zagrebu i do 50 km od grada; izvan Zagreba naplaćujemo 0,90 € po kilometru.

2. **Give each page question-style H2s.** Examples: "Koliko košta…?", "Što je uključeno?", "Koliko traje…?", "Što trebam pripremiti?". Put a 40–80-word answer under each, and add 3–5 page-specific FAQs with FAQPage markup.

3. **Publish the facts competitors win on.** Only the owner knows these, so use real numbers:
   - van model, cargo dimensions and volume in m³, payload in kg, number of vans
   - insurance: who the insurer is and how much the policy covers
   - minimum billing time and any call-out fee
   - typical job durations, e.g. "garsonijera: 2–3 sata"

4. **Add a dedicated `/cjenik` page.** Include a price table and 3–4 worked examples: garsonijera, dvosoban stan, IKEA delivery, and 1 m³ of bulky waste. Competitors' price pages are what engines cite for price questions.

5. **Write guides for high-intent questions.** Each needs numbers and a clear answer:
   - **"Besplatni odvoz glomaznog otpada u Zagrebu ili privatni odvoz?"** Explain the city's free quota through Čistoća and when a paid same-day pickup makes sense. Search summaries describe the quota as twice a year up to 2 m³ or once a year up to 4 m³; check the current rules before publishing. Honest comparisons get cited and build trust.
   - **"Koliko košta selidba u Zagrebu 2026."**
   - **"Koliko stane u kombi?"**, mapping van volume to typical flat sizes.
   - **"Dostava iz IKEA-e Zagreb isti dan".** kombi-dostava.hr appears for this query with a dedicated page.

6. **Differentiate the three overlapping "kombi prijevoz" pages.** `/kombi-prijevoz`, `/kombi-prijevoz-zagreb` and `/povoljan-kombi-prijevoz` say the same thing three ways. Give each distinct facts; for example, `/kombi-prijevoz-zagreb` could cover districts, parking and pedestrian-zone access, and typical in-city durations. The alternative is to merge them, but GitHub Pages can't issue 301 redirects, so a merged page needs a meta-refresh stub plus canonical.

7. **Add text to `/kako-do-nas`.** Crawlers can't read a map iframe. Describe landmarks, parking and the nearest tram or bus stop. If customers don't actually visit the address, say so.

8. **Add a small English section.** For "man with a van Zagreb", web search returned UK-based international movers, Eurosender and a Europe-wide man-and-van site, but no local Zagreb firm. Expats, students and tourists ask AI assistants in English, and the domain is already English. Pages for man with a van, moving, furniture delivery and rubbish removal under `/en/`, with prices, would face little competition. Add `hreflang` pairs when you do.

## 4. Trust and consistency

AI systems cross-check claims. Contradictions lower their confidence, and the wrong version can be repeated to customers. The deployed site contains:

| Claim | Where | Conflicts with |
|---|---|---|
| "S preko 30 godina iskustva" and "više od tri desetljeća" | `src/views/KombiPrijevozView.vue:11`, `src/views/PovoljanKombiPrijevozView.vue:5` | "20+ godina iskustva" in the homepage hero and About section |
| "Za hitne prijevoze… 24/7" | `src/views/KombiPrijevozZagrebView.vue:28` | "svaki dan 08–20 h" everywhere else, and `openingHours` in the schema |
| "Radimo vikendom, praznicima i izvan uobičajenog radnog vremena" | `src/views/KombiDostavaView.vue:16-17` | the same 08–20 h hours |
| "Odbacili smo rigidne cjenike" | `src/views/PovoljanKombiPrijevozView.vue:12` | the price list published on the homepage (`src/data/pricing.ts`) |
| Credit cards accepted | `index.html:104` | "Gotovinom ili internet bankarstvom" (`src/data/faq.ts:20`) |

Choose the true value for each, define it once in `src/data/site.ts`, and reference it everywhere so it can't drift again.

### Other trust gaps

- **Unsupported superlatives.**
  - `/specijalni-prijevoz` describes concert pianos lifted out by crane, art moved in climate-controlled transport, and safes over a tonne (`SpecijalniPrijevozView.vue:12-14`).
  - `/kombi-prijevoz` calls the company "lideri u području profesionalnog prijevoza" (`KombiPrijevozView.vue:26`).
  - The appliance and office-move pages mention "tehničari" who install appliances and network cabling.

  If these are real, back them up with photos, dates and specifics. If not, remove them. An AI engine repeating an inflated claim to a customer is worse than no mention.
- **Testimonials can't be verified.** There are four quotes signed with a first name and initial, with no date or source. One calls the company "Kombi prijevoza" (`TestimonialsSection.vue:11`). Replace them with linked Google reviews once the profile has some.
- **No About page or real people.** Add `/o-nama` with the owner's name and photo, founding year, team, vehicles, insurance, and the legal entity name and OIB. For a name as generic as "Kombi Transport", legal name plus OIB is the strongest way for Google and AI systems to match the site to registry records such as sudreg, poslovna.hr and fininfo.hr. Croatian business websites are usually required to publish these details anyway, so it's worth confirming.
- **No email address and no privacy policy.** The contact form collects names, phone numbers, emails, addresses and company OIBs. Add both.

The search tool's own AI summary, a reasonable proxy for how answer engines compose responses, quoted competitors' figures for every non-brand query and never mentioned Kombi Transport.

"Kombi Transport" literally means "van transport". It's the same phrase dozens of Zagreb van services use to describe themselves, and similarly named sites exist elsewhere. Engines need off-site corroboration before treating it as a distinct entity, and right now there is almost none.

### Recommendations (owner tasks, no code)

1. **Google Business Profile.** Confirm it exists and is complete:
   - a moving-company primary category, plus relevant secondary ones for delivery and rubbish removal
   - the same name, address, phone and hours as the site
   - photos of the vans, and services listed with prices

   Ask every customer for a review, for example with a QR code on the invoice. Google AI Overviews and Gemini lean on this data.
2. **Bing Places.** Claim it; it can import from the Google profile. ChatGPT search and Copilot draw on Bing's local data.
3. **Apple Business Connect,** for Apple Maps and Siri.
4. **Directories and quote marketplaces where competitors appear:** preporuka.hr, kombiprijevoz.hr, kombizagreb.com, hr.sirelo.org and emajstor.hr. Use the same name, address and phone everywhere.
5. **Facebook.** The site links to a share short-link (`src/data/site.ts:7`). Use the canonical page URL in links and in `sameAs`. A page at `facebook.com/p/Kombi-Transport-100063097287222/` appeared in search; if it's yours, use that one and enable reviews.
6. **Earned mentions.** Furniture and appliance shops that recommend delivery partners, local news and neighbourhood portals, and partnerships with renovation contractors (for šuta removal). One real mention on a trusted Croatian site is worth more than many directory entries.
